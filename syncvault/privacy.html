SyncVault Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

SyncVault backs up folders from your Android phone to a server you set up and control, over SFTP. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: the developer never receives any of your data.

Data the developer collects

None. SyncVault has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and it doesn't contact any server run by the developer or by third parties.

Data sent to your server

When you run a backup, Preview or Test connection, SyncVault connects only to the server you entered. It sends:

  • copies of the files in the phone folders you chose to back up, along with their names, sizes and modification times;
  • the user name you entered, and an SSH login made with the phone's key.

Everything is sent over an encrypted SSH connection. What happens to the files on that server is up to you, the person who runs it.

Data stored on your phone

  • Settings: the server address, port, user name and backup folder, your schedule and options, and the server's host key that you trusted.
  • The phone's SSH key: an Ed25519 key pair created by the app. The private key never leaves the phone. You copy only the public key to your server.
  • Folder access: read-only permission, granted through Android's folder picker, to the folders you chose. SyncVault never changes or deletes files on your phone.

This data stays in the app's private storage and is left out of Android's cloud backups. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Network access: to connect to your server, and to check that you're on Wi-Fi.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show backup progress and results.
  • Alarms and running at startup: to start scheduled backups on time, including after a restart.

Sharing

The developer doesn't sell or share data with anyone, because the developer doesn't have any. Your files go only to the server you set up.

Children

SyncVault isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
