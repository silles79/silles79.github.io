TorqueLink Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

TorqueLink is an Android app for car diagnostics, off-road tools and an offline weather station. It is made by an independent developer ("I" below).

The short version

TorqueLink collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It doesn't ask Android for internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere by itself. Everything it records stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

What the app uses, and why

Bluetooth. To connect to the OBD-II adapter you pick, and to read data from your car through it. The app only reads from the car, except when you ask it to clear trouble codes.

Location. Used only:

  • to calibrate the altitude reading from GPS, when you tap to do so;
  • on Android 11 and older, because Android requires location permission to find Bluetooth devices.

Your location is not stored, apart from the altitude result, and never leaves the phone.

Motion sensors and barometer. For the inclinometer, compass, G-meter, altitude, climb counter and weather station. Readings are processed on the phone.

Notifications and running in the background. The climb counter and the weather station, when you turn them on, show a notification and keep working while the app is closed. The weather station restarts its readings after the phone reboots.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your settings, such as units, calibrations and warning angles.
  • Trip figures.
  • The last car's VIN (vehicle identification number), so the app knows which car it is connected to.
  • Weather station readings, kept for 7 days and then deleted.

All of this stays in the app's private storage. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

When you share something

Some screens have buttons to share a log, the diagnostics report, trouble codes, weather readings or a feature request. These open Android's share sheet or your email app, and nothing is sent until you choose where it goes and send it yourself. The diagnostics report can include your car's VIN and the adapter's details. If you send it to me, I use it only to fix problems and improve the app, I don't pass it on, and I'll delete it on request.

Third parties

TorqueLink contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking. It is installed through Google Play, which collects its own data under Google's privacy policy.

Supporting TorqueLink. The optional support purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. TorqueLink only asks the Play Store app on your phone whether it has been bought; it never sees your payment details. As with any purchase on Google Play, Google gives me an order record, which I use only for refunds and support. The Play Billing library's own usage logging is left out of TorqueLink, so it doesn't send anything either.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
